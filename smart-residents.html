Privacy Notice

Privacy & POPIA Notice

Who processes your information

When you apply for a property on this app, your personal information is processed by the landlord who owns that listing (the "responsible party" under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013, "POPIA"), for the purpose of assessing your rental application.

What we collect & why

Identity details (name, ID/passport number), contact details, current address, employment and income information, next-of-kin details, and move-in preferences — collected solely to assess your suitability as a tenant, verify your identity and ability to pay rent, and to prepare a lease if approved. We only ask for the filenames of supporting documents (ID copy, payslips); this demo does not upload or store the documents themselves.

Where it's stored

This application currently stores data in your browser's local storage on your own device, and in the landlord's browser once they view your application — not on a central server. It is not sold, and is not shared with any third party beyond the landlord you applied to.

How long we keep it

Rejected applications are automatically removed from local storage after 90 days. Approved applications and signed leases are kept for as long as reasonably needed for the tenancy and any legal recordkeeping requirement. You can request deletion at any time (see below).

Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to know what information is held about you, to request a copy of it, to request correction of inaccurate information, and to request its deletion (subject to any legal requirement to retain it). Use "My Application" to look up your application by phone, ID number, or reference code, then download or delete your data directly.

Consent

By ticking the consent box when applying, you agree to the landlord processing your information as described here, including verifying your employment, income, and identity, and conducting a reasonable affordability/background check.

Complaints

If you believe your information has been mishandled, you may raise this with the landlord directly, or with South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Known limitation

This is a lightweight demo application without a central server. While we minimise what's collected, apply access controls in the interface, and give you deletion rights, information stored in browser local storage is not encrypted at rest. Please avoid using shared or public devices when applying, and use "My Application" to delete your data once your tenancy question is resolved.
